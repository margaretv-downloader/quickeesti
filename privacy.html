QuickEesti — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

QuickEesti is a free, non-commercial app for learning Estonian, made for family members and friends. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What we collect

  • Google account basics — when you choose “Sign in with Google”, we receive your name, e-mail address and a Google account identifier. We use them only to identify your account and keep your learning progress.
  • Learning progress — completed lessons, review (flashcard) progress, daily streak, placement test result and optional reward settings.

Without signing in, all progress stays only on your device.

What we do NOT do

  • No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • We do not access your contacts, files, location, camera or microphone.

Where data is stored

Progress is stored on your device and, if you sign in, in a database hosted by Supabase (EU region). Each account can read and change only its own data (row-level security). Text-to-speech requests send lesson phrases (not personal data) to the public Estonian speech service of TartuNLP / University of Tartu.

Your rights

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and all stored progress, contact the developer at the address below — data is deleted within 30 days. Android may also back up app data to your own Google account (Android Backup); you can manage this in your phone settings.

Children

The app is intended for family use; children use it with their parents’ consent.

Contact

Developer contact: margaret.baldina@gmail.com

Политика конфиденциальности (RU)

  • Что мы получаем: при входе через Google — имя, e-mail и идентификатор аккаунта; а также прогресс обучения (пройденные уроки, повторение слов, серия дней, результат теста уровня, настройки поощрения). Без входа прогресс хранится только на телефоне.
